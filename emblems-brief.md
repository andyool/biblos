# Biblos emblems: image brief

The emblems are the badges you wear: on your profile, in the top bar, and on the path cards. The app draws a round badge behind each one in the theme's colours, so these images are the object alone.

## Style guide (applies to every image)

- Deliver a 1024 x 1024 PNG with a fully transparent background. The object is centred and fills about 80% of the canvas on its longest side, upright, the same scale across the whole set. No badge, ring, disc, backdrop, shadow or glow: just the object.
- Same metal as the seals: sculpted antique gold in high relief, highlights around #f1cd66, recesses deep umber around #2a1a02, lit softly from the top-left with a satin sheen. No sparkles, lens flare, rainbow reflections or enamel. It must read on both a navy (#0a0f1d) and a parchment (#f1e6cb) background.
- These are shown as small as 38 pixels, so they are simpler than the seals: one object, thick solid forms, no thin lines, no interior texture, no fine detail. If it would not read on a coin, leave it out.
- Viewed straight on and flat. No text, letters or numbers.
- Save each as the filename in brackets. One version per emblem; the app greys out unowned ones itself.

## The emblems

1. Cross [cross.png] — A plain Latin cross, the upright taller than the arms, ends slightly flared. Thick enough to hold at a glance.
2. Dove [dove.png] — A dove in flight seen from the side, wings raised high, a single small leaf in its beak.
3. Lamp [lamp.png] — A clay oil lamp in profile with a looped handle at the back and one flame at the spout.
4. Ichthys [ichthys.png] — The two-arc fish, the arcs crossing to form the tail, drawn as one thick ribbon of gold. Nothing inside it.
5. Olive Branch [olive.png] — A single olive sprig on a gently curved stem, leaves in pairs, two olives near the tip.
6. Anchor [anchor.png] — A ship's anchor standing upright: ring at the top, a straight crossbar, two curved flukes at the bottom.
7. Shepherd's Crook [crook.png] — A shepherd's staff standing nearly upright, with a deep curled hook at the top.
8. Scroll [scroll.png] — A scroll partly unrolled, both rods showing, the sheet curling over at top and bottom.
9. Sheaf [wheat.png] — Three ears of wheat bound with a twist of cord, their heads fanning out at the top.
10. True Vine [vine.png] — A short length of vine with one broad leaf, one small cluster of grapes, and a curling tendril.
11. Keys [key.png] — Two old keys crossed in an X, round bows at the top, teeth at the bottom.
12. Quill [quill.png] — A single feather quill angled as if writing, nib at the lower left, the plume arching up to the right.
13. Shield of Faith [shield.png] — A tall kite-shaped shield with a small cross at its centre and no other marks.
14. Flame [flame.png] — One upright flame with two licking tongues, standing alone with no lamp or candle.
15. David's Harp [harp.png] — A small lyre with a curved frame and six strings, seen straight on.
16. Cup of Blessing [chalice.png] — A tall-stemmed cup with a wide bowl and a round foot, seen straight on.
17. Heart [heart.png] — A full, rounded heart in deep relief, plain.
18. Chi-Rho [chirho.png] — The Chi-Rho monogram: an X with a tall P rising through its centre, strokes thick and even.
19. Morning Star [star.png] — An eight-pointed star, four long points and four short between them, bright at the centre.
20. Sun of Righteousness [sun.png] — A full sun disc with twelve short straight rays evenly spaced around it.
21. Crown of Life [crown.png] — A simple crown with three points, each tipped with a bead, on a plain band.
