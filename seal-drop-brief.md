# Seal drop: asset brief

## How it will play

1. The page dims. The new seal appears huge and soft-focused high above, then drops with real gravity in about half a second, a shadow growing on the ground where it will land.
2. Impact. A gold-white flash, the whole app jolts down and sideways, and a crack spreads out from the landing point across the screen in a third of a second. Shards, dust and gold sparks fly.
3. The seal rocks once and settles in the crater. Its name and talents fade in beneath it. A tap clears the crack and carries on.

The fall, shadow, flash, screen shake, crack spread, spark bursts and settle are all code. The images below are the parts worth drawing properly. If the phone asks for reduced motion, the seal simply appears with its name.

## Style guide (applies to every image)

- Transparent PNG, no background, no vignette, no colour wash. Everything that is not the object itself is fully transparent, since the app dims and tints the page behind it.
- Drawn to sit over both a navy (#0a0f1d) and a parchment (#f1e6cb) page. Use pale glass whites and light greys for the crack and shards, with a hair-thin dark edge so they still read on parchment.
- Lit from the top-left, the same as the seals. No lens flare, no text, no logos.
- Save each as the filename in brackets. I will compress them.

## The assets

**1. The crack** [crack-1.png, crack-2.png, crack-3.png] — Required. Three variants, portrait 1080 x 2340. A shattered-glass crack radiating from a single impact point placed exactly at the horizontal centre, 42% of the way down. A dense web of fine fractures and a few small crazed chips near the centre, thinning to five or six long cracks that run toward the edges and fade out fully before reaching them. Lines only, white to pale grey with slight refraction highlights. No crater, no hole, no seal: the seal lands on top.

**2. Glass shards** [shard-1.png to shard-8.png] — Recommended. Eight separate fragments, 256 x 256 each, one shard per file, centred. Jagged triangular and slivered pieces of clear glass with a bright edge and faint grey body, each a different shape, all the same material. They are thrown from the impact point in code.

**3. Dust puff** [dust.png] — Optional. One soft, round cloud of pale stone dust, 512 x 512, feathered to transparent at the edge, denser at the bottom. Used once per impact, scaled up and faded in code.

**4. Impact sound** [impact.mp3] — Optional. Under one second: a heavy stone thud with a short glass crack right after, no reverb tail. Mono, 44.1 kHz. The app makes its other sounds in code, so this would be its first audio file; if you skip it I will synthesise a thud.

The seal itself comes from the seals brief (seals-brief.md); nothing extra is needed for it.
