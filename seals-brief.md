# Biblos seals: image brief

## Style guide (applies to every image)

- Deliver a 1024 x 1024 PNG with a fully transparent background. The seal is a perfect circle, centred, filling about 90% of the canvas. Nothing outside the circle: no drop shadow, no backdrop, no glow spilling past the rim.
- Every seal is the same object: a round medallion of cast antique gold with a rim of small raised beads, a slightly recessed face with a faint radial brushed texture, and one motif sculpted in high relief at the centre. Same rim, same proportions, every time.
- The motif is the same gold as the medallion, embossed with real depth, catching light on its upper-left edges and falling into shadow on the lower-right. Not painted, not coloured, not flat line art.
- Aged gold throughout. Highlights around #f1cd66, recesses deep umber around #2a1a02, lit softly from the top-left with a satin sheen. No sparkles, lens flare, rainbow reflections or enamel. It must sit on both a navy (#0a0f1d) and a parchment (#f1e6cb) background.
- One motif per seal, a bold simple silhouette filling about 60% of the face, viewed straight on. It must read at 74 pixels wide, so no fine texture or clutter. Where a description places something (lower third, left of centre), follow it; that keeps the set balanced.
- No text, letters, numbers or dates anywhere. Where a scroll or page shows writing, use faint wavy relief lines, never real letters.
- Save each as the filename in brackets. One version per seal; the app greys out locked ones itself.

## The seals

1. First Light [first_light.png] — Half a sun breaking over a razor-straight horizon set in the lower third. Its rays are long and straight, alternating long and short, and fan up to fill the upper face.
2. The Sower [sower.png] — A hand seen from the side, palm open and turned down, releasing seeds that arc down to three furrows along the bottom. Five seeds, all in mid-air, large enough to count.
3. First Fruits [fruit_1.png] — One heavy cluster of large round grapes hanging from a curled vine tendril, a single broad leaf half covering the top of the cluster. The cluster fills the centre.
4. Treasure Hidden [heart_1.png] — A thick heart in deep relief with a tiny keyhole cut into its centre, a faint glow escaping the keyhole. Nothing else on the face.
5. Fruitful Branch [fruit_10.png] — An olive branch sweeping in one curve from lower left to upper right, long narrow leaves and ten plump olives spaced along it.
6. Treasury of Ten [heart_10.png] — A small heart at the centre, surrounded by a neat ring of ten coins that overlap slightly like a necklace. Each coin bears a tiny star.
7. Crown of Life [heart_25.png] — A tall royal crown seen straight on, five points each tipped with a jewel, a band of pearls around the base. It fills the face.
8. Living Library [heart_50.png] — A wall of square pigeonholes, five across and four high, each holding the round end of a rolled scroll seen head-on. It reads as a honeycomb of circles inside squares.
9. Psalmist [heart_100.png] — A tall lyre-shaped harp with ten taut strings, its frame curling at the top, a single small star hanging above the highest string.
10. Kindled [streak_3.png] — A low clay oil lamp in profile, a single small flame just caught at its spout, three tiny sparks rising above it.
11. A Lamp to My Feet [streak_7.png] — An oil lamp hanging from a short chain in the upper half, a cone of light falling onto two bare footprints on a stone path in the lower half.
12. Keeper of the Flame [streak_30.png] — Two hands cupped together, fingers curled, holding a tall steady flame that rises above the fingertips. The hands fill the lower two-thirds, the flame reaches toward the rim.
13. Watchman [streak_100.png] — A square stone watchtower with battlements, a brazier blazing on top, set slightly right of centre. A thin crescent moon hangs low to its left.
14. A Year of Faithfulness [streak_365.png] — A closed laurel wreath ringing the face just inside the beads, twelve leaves a side. Inside it, an upright torch with a full flame.
15. Without Blemish [perfect_1.png] — One large flawless pearl sitting in an open scallop shell, the shell's ridges fanning out behind it. The pearl is smooth and bright against the ridged shell.
16. Refined as Gold [perfect_25.png] — A crucible held in tongs, tipped so a thick stream of molten gold pours into a rectangular ingot mould, flames licking the crucible's base. The pour is the brightest thing on the seal.
17. Daily Bread [goal_7.png] — A round domed loaf with a deep cross scored into its crust, three ears of wheat fanned out behind it like a crest.
18. Pilgrim [path_any.png] — A tall walking staff planted in the foreground, a road winding away behind it in three bends to a small arched gate near the top of the face.
19. The Good Shepherd [path_shepherd.png] — A shepherd's crook standing upright, its hook curling to the left, beside a still pool drawn as three calm horizontal ripples. A tuft of grass at its foot.
20. Teach Us to Pray [path_prayer.png] — Two hands pressed together in prayer, fingers pointing up, three thin rays rising from the fingertips toward the rim.
21. Fully Armed [path_armour.png] — A tall kite-shaped shield bearing a cross, a sword crossed diagonally behind it with the hilt showing top right, and a crested helmet resting on the shield's top edge.
22. Morning Watch [morning.png] — A cockerel in profile, chest out, head thrown back mid-crow, standing on a short fence rail. A small sun rises behind its tail.
23. Night Watch [night.png] — A single candle in a stick with a loop handle, its flame tall and still. A crescent moon above it to the left, three small stars to the right.
24. Diligent Scribe [scribe.png] — A quill mid-stroke on a partly unrolled scroll, its rods at top and bottom, an inkpot to the left. The nib touches a line of wavy relief writing.
25. Take Up and Read [reader_1.png] — A single open book seen from slightly above, pages fanned, a ribbon marker hanging from the spine, a short beam of light rising straight up from the gutter.
26. Like the Bereans [reader_30.png] — Three open books stacked at slight angles, a round reading lens with a short handle resting on the top page.
27. Delight in the Law [reader_100.png] — A broad fruit tree with a thick trunk and round fruit among the leaves, its roots exposed and reaching into a stream that runs across the bottom in three curved lines.
28. Write the Vision [journal_1.png] — A rectangular stone tablet standing upright, a five-pointed star carved at its top and three plain horizontal lines beneath, a stylus leaning against its right edge.
29. Pondered in the Heart [journal_10.png] — An open book resting inside a large heart outline, the heart's point at the bottom of the face, the pages filling its centre.
30. Book of Remembrance [journal_7.png] — A thick closed book seen from the front cover, a metal clasp on its edge, a seven-pointed star in relief at the centre of the cover.
31. Ebenezer [answered_1.png] — A tall standing stone, slightly tapered, set upright on a small cairn of five rounded stones, grass at the base.
32. Whole Counsel [book_1.png] — A scroll fully unrolled, both wooden rods bare at far left and right, the sheet stretched between them in a shallow curve with faint relief lines of writing.
33. Treasure Seeker [own_verse.png] — An open treasure chest with its lid thrown back, a single rolled scroll inside, a hanging lantern above casting a cone of light into the chest.
34. Talents Multiplied [xp_1000.png] — A pyramid of coins, four at the base rising to one at the top, each stamped with a star, the top coin catching the light brightest.
35. Good and Faithful [xp_10000.png] — A pair of tall arched gates, both doors swung open toward the viewer, light pouring through the gap in long straight rays.
